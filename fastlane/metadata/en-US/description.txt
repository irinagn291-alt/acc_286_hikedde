Сhikedde is a daily bead diary. Each day you tie a short caption on today's bead, then string a photo or a voice note so that day stays on a vertical thread. It is for people who keep days as knots on a string, not as a reverse-chronological feed.

• Tie a caption on today's bead (Tie, field Caption)
• One knot per calendar day unless you Peel
• String a photo onto a Tied knot (String, then Photo)
• String a voice note onto a Tied knot (String, then Voice, then Stop & String)
• Play or stop a strung voice note (Play voice note / Stop voice note)
• Peel the newest action
• Vertical thread of earlier beads, newest open bead on top
• On this day: today's knot beside strung knots from prior years on the same month and day
• Weeks: year cloth of ISO weeks, with Tension streak and totals
• Gallery: every strung photo or voice note
• Thread: one earlier bead, large
• Saved. after a successful Tie or String
• Export thread and Export weft from Settings
• Haptics on or off
• Show introduction to replay onboarding
• Reset thread to delete the thread and its media
• Contact for support
• Dark appearance only

Captions, photos, and voice notes stay on this device.
